Keeping your helper safe, in plain words

  1. It still follows the rules. It never asks for your passwords, codes, or money.
  2. You know it’s really your helper. You open it yourself from the app or link you saved, never from a message someone sent you.
  3. Its tips stay current. Scams and settings change over time, so it helps when your helper’s tips keep up.

You stay in control. You can stop anytime.

What your helper does, and never does

Your helper does

guides checks you do yourself · explains each step in plain words · goes at your pace · lets you stop anytime

It never

asks for your passwords or codes · asks for remote access · logs in to your accounts · rushes you · asks you to move money or pay with gift cards

You always sign in yourself, on the official website or app.

Ask your helper one question

One check for today is plenty.

  1. Open your Grok Bot yourself, from the app or the link you saved.
  2. Type: What will you never ask me for?
  3. Compare its answer with the list above.
