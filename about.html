About SecureWithGrok

SecureWithGrok offers calm, plain-language guides that help everyday people protect their important accounts, like email, one small step at a time.

Each guide points you to the official website or app for that account. You follow the steps yourself and decide what to change and when.

If you'd like a hand, an optional Grok Bot can walk you through a guide, explain confusing screens, and help you keep track of what you've done. It never needs your passwords.

What we believe

  • No scare tactics. Nothing here is urgent.
  • We never ask for your passwords or sign-in codes.
  • Official sources first, explained in everyday words.

You can find us at securewithgrok.com and securewithgrok.ai.
