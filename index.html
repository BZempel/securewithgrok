How we keep it trustworthy

Our guides point you to official sources: the real settings pages of your email provider, your bank, and the Social Security Administration. We explain what each step does in everyday words, without scare tactics.

Grok Bots are here to help, never to pressure. They won't rush you, and you decide what to change and when.
